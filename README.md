FDA Capstone - Vendor Contract Risk Reviewer
Problem
Hospital legal teams spend significant time manually reviewing vendor contracts. Risky clauses related to liability, data protection, and auto-renewal may be missed.

Goal
Build an AI-assisted reviewer that:

Extracts clauses
Compares clauses against the legal playbook
Identifies deviations
Assigns risk levels
Generates a negotiation memo
Requires lawyer approval
Scope
In Scope
Clause extraction
Clause classification
Playbook comparison
Risk scoring
Negotiation memo generation
Out of Scope
E-signature
Editing contract files
Real contracts
KPIs
Reduce review time from 3 hours to 30 minutes
90% deviation recall
Zero uncited findings
